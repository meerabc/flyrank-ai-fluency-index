## Note on duplicated capstone briefs

"General AI Fluency · Impact Project" and "Send the Link: Launch, Demo &
Story" carry an identical brief to a third card, "The Plan to Keep
Building." All three ask for the same thing: a next-case note, a named
next piece of work, and evidence a reminder was set. This looks like a
portal content duplication rather than three genuinely distinct
requirements. The same real materials were submitted honestly to each,
and this has been flagged in a Q&A session for confirmation.
